Reverse Clip v0.5.0 — AAC Audio Modes development in progress (NOT stable).
Reverse and Ping-pong silent H.264 MP4 are verified in desktop ST/MT Chrome.
An audio selector defaults to NONE and remains disabled for the existing
pinned v0.4 cores. Builder AAC audio-segment support is under test; a real
A/V join, sound waveform, synchronization and click/restart tests are still
required before audio playback/export may be enabled.
Never claim audio works merely because an AAC encoder was compiled.
WASM Actions artifact expires 2026-10-20; stable GPL corresponding sources
must be shipped before public distribution. Browser Kitty integration deferred.
